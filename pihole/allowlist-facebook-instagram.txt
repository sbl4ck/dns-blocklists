! Title: Allowlist - Facebook comments, Facebook/Instagram video, Instagram DMs (Pi-hole v6)
! Description: Unblocks the Meta domains needed for Facebook comments (including the embedded comments plugin on other sites), Facebook/Instagram video playback and Instagram/Messenger direct messages. Tracking/ad hosts (ads., pixel., analytics., an., ep*. facebook.com, l.facebook.com) are deliberately NOT allowed.
! Syntax: AdBlock
! Usage: add in Pi-hole as an ALLOWLIST (Lists -> Add allowlist), then Tools -> Update Gravity.
!
